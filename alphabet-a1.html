The French letter names, with a rough English sound-alike for each. The letters themselves are the same as in English — only the names change.

Les pièges pour anglophones. French g sounds like English "j" (jay), and French j sounds like English "g" (gee) — they swap. French e ("uh") vs i ("ee") vs a ("ah") also collide with the English names. And u is its own sound — round your lips tightly while saying "ee".
