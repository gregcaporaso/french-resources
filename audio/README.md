## alphabet-fr.ogg / alphabet-fr.mp3

A native speaker reciting the 26 letters of the French alphabet (~26 seconds), used on `alphabet-a1.html`.

- Source: [File:French Grammar - The French Alphabet.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:French_Grammar_-_The_French_Alphabet.ogg) on Wikimedia Commons, recorded for the French Wikibook (alphabet content by Wikibooks User:Hashar; uploaded by Hagindaz).
